## Oracle Database Schema

> ### ⚠️ This document uses the **retired** `WCSEXP_*` vocabulary
>
> Everything below is as the extract environment presented it: a set of `WCSEXP_*` views in the
> `APPS` schema, one per real EBS table. **Those views are no longer used.** The SQL in
> `data/sql/` and `data/sql/turso/queries/` reads the base tables directly, and the `WCSEXP_*`
> compatibility views in `data/sql/turso/00-schema.sql` have been retired.
>
> The **column lists below are still accurate** — the views were near-pure projections, so the
> columns are the tables' columns under their real names. Only the object names changed. Read
> each `### WCSEXP_X` heading as "the table behind `WCSEXP_X`", per this map:
>
> | In this document | Base table to use |
> |---|---|
> | `WCSEXP_GL_CODE_COMBINATIONS` | `GL_CODE_COMBINATIONS` |
> | `WCSEXP_GL_BALANCES` | `GL_BALANCES` — **`SET_OF_BOOKS_ID` is `LEDGER_ID`** |
> | `WCSEXP_GL_JE_HEADERS` | `GL_JE_HEADERS` |
> | `WCSEXP_GL_JE_LINES` | `GL_JE_LINES` |
> | `WCSEXP_GL_LOOKUPS` | `GL_LOOKUPS` |
> | `WCSEXP_FND_ID_FLEX_STRUCTURES` | `FND_ID_FLEX_STRUCTURES` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_HEADERS` | `PO_HEADERS_ALL` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_LINES` | `PO_LINES_ALL` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_LINE_LOCATIONS` | `PO_LINE_LOCATIONS_ALL` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_DISTRIBUTIONS` | `PO_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_VENDORS` | `PO_VENDORS` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_VENDOR_SITES` | `PO_VENDOR_SITES_ALL` |
> | `WCSEXP_PO_LINE_TYPES` | `PO_LINE_TYPES` |
> | `WCSEXP_AP_INVOICES` | `AP_INVOICES_ALL` |
> | `WCSEXP_AP_INV_LINES` | `AP_INV_LINES` — **the PO-link columns listed here are `NULL` in the view; the real link is on `AP_INVOICE_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL`** |
> | `WCSEXP_AP_INV_DISTRIBUTIONS` | `AP_INVOICE_DISTRIBUTIONS_ALL` — **`DIST_CODE_COMBINATION_ID` is `CODE_COMBINATION_ID`; `POSTED_FLAG` does not exist** |
> | `WCSEXP_AP_INVOICE_PAYMENTS` | `AP_INVOICE_PAYMENTS_ALL` — **`PAYMENT_NUM` is `CHECK_NUMBER`** |
> | `WCSEXP_AP_CHECKS` | `AP_INVOICE_PAYMENTS_ALL` — **there is no `AP_CHECKS_ALL`; `CHECK_DATE` is `PAYMENT_DATE`** |
>
> Three objects listed below — `WCSEXP_PO_RELEASES`, `WCSEXP_PO_VENDOR_CONTACTS` and
> `WCSEXP_HR_LOCATIONS` — were never extracted and have no counterpart in this repo.
> `PO_LOOKUP_CODES`, `PO_AGENTS` and `PA_*` are in `../oracle/db-schema.md`'s sibling notes and in
> the DBA's grant list; see `data/reports/report-findings.md` for the environment findings.
